# Maps

Maps made for Timber Empires. Subscribe on the Steam Workshop and they show up in the New Game map list.

## Timberborn Nothing

[![Timberborn Nothing](assets/maps/timberborn-nothing.jpg)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3818767697)

A 256x256 PvP map with forest on over half the tiles, inspired by Forest Nothing from Age of Empires II. [Get it on the Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3818767697).
