# Troops

Every troop has 100 health. Damage is per hour of fighting, before the multipliers in [Combat](../military/combat.md). "Damage taken" multiplies every blow the troop takes: lower is tougher. Walk speed is against a beaver's normal pace.

## Ranks

A troop earns points for each enemy it brings down, more for a dangerous one and less once it's a veteran itself.

| Rank | Points | Damage | Damage taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| Veteran | 20 | +10% | -7% |
| Elite | 50 | +20% | -14% |
| Hero | 100 | +30% | -21% |
