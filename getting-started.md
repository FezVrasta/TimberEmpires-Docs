# Getting started

## What you need

- Timberborn 1.1 or newer.
- [BeaverBuddies Co-Op/PvP Edition](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813984327). Not the original BeaverBuddies: they share a mod ID, but only this edition has what Timber Empires hooks into, and with the original a multiplayer game goes out of sync. Timber Empires tells you on the main menu when it finds the original.
- Harmony and Mod Settings (1.1 or newer).

Subscribing on the Steam Workshop pulls in the mods it needs. Every player needs the same mods, in the same versions.

## Playing with others

Timber Empires runs on top of BeaverBuddies, so hosting and joining work the way BeaverBuddies does: Multiplayer on the main menu, then host a new game, host a saved one, join a friend, or find a match.

**Matchmaking.** Find a match pairs you with another player over Steam. Each of you picks a faction, the host's map is used, and every player places their own first District Center wherever they want on it. That first one is free: it's built at once, with 7 adults, 3 children, 100 berries and 100 water.

**Hosting a game.** In a game you host yourself, the map's starting District Center belongs to nobody until someone takes it in its panel ("Owned by"). Once a district has an owner, it stays theirs. With [Mixed factions](settings.md#mixed-factions) on, each player plays their own faction (set "My faction" before joining) and a player whose faction isn't on the map yet gets the same free first District Center.

**Factions.** Folktails and Iron Teeth each have their own troops and a few buildings of their own. Factions from other mods work too: they're built on one of the two and get its buildings and troops (see [Modding](modding.md)).

## Playing alone

Without other players, turn on [Play alone as an empire](settings.md#play-alone-as-an-empire) (it's on by default). Your districts are yours, land claims and fog of war work as they do against other players, and the bandits are your opponents. It needs the Co-Op/PvP Edition installed even for single player.

With it off, Timber Empires' buildings and troops are still there, but there's no fog, no land claims and no ownership.

## First steps

1. Put up a [Watchtower](playing/territory.md#land-claims) or two at the edge of what you want: claimed land is land nobody else can build on.
2. Build a [Forge](military/index.md#weapons) and a [Barracks](military/index.md#training-troops). Spearmen and archers need no science.
3. Scout the [bandit camps](playing/bandits.md) near you. From the third cycle they start raiding.
4. Pick a [district specialization](playing/economy.md#district-specializations) once you know what each district is for.
