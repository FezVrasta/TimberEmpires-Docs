# Getting started

## What you need

- Timberborn 1.1 or newer.
- [BeaverBuddies Co-Op/PvP Edition](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813984327) (also on [mod.io](https://mod.io/g/timberborn/m/beaverbuddies-co-oppvp-edition)), for multiplayer, AI opponents, and owned districts and fog of war in a game alone. Without it you can still play a game alone with Timber Empires' buildings. The original BeaverBuddies shares its mod ID, but only this edition has what Timber Empires hooks into, and with the original a multiplayer game goes out of sync. Timber Empires warns you on the main menu when it finds the original.
- Harmony and Mod Settings (1.1 or newer).

[Subscribe to Timber Empires on the Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3814012422) (also on [mod.io](https://mod.io/g/timberborn/m/timber-empires)) and it pulls in the mods it needs. Every player needs the same mods, in the same versions.

## Playing with others

Each multiplayer game has a game mode (Co-op, PvE, PvP or Horde), picked on the last page of the New Game screens: see [Game modes](game-modes.md).

Hosting and joining work the way they do in BeaverBuddies: Multiplayer on the main menu, then host a new game, host a saved one, join a friend, or start a Quick match.

**Quick match.** Pairs you with another player over Steam. Each of you picks a faction, the host's map is used, and each of you places your own first District Center anywhere on it. That first one is free: it's built at once, with the beavers, food and water the game's difficulty starts you with.

**Hosting a game.** The map's starting District Center belongs to nobody until someone takes it in its panel ("Owned by"). Each player plays their own faction: pick it in the lobby before the host starts, or set My faction in BeaverBuddies' mod settings. A player whose faction isn't on the map yet gets the same free first District Center.

**Factions.** Folktails and Iron Teeth each have their own troops and a few buildings of their own. Factions from other mods work too: they're built on one of the two and get its buildings and troops (see [Modding](modding/index.md)).

## Playing alone

Pick a mode on the New Game screens' last page. PvE, PvP and Horde give you your own districts, land claims and fog of war, with the bandits, AI opponents or the undead as the enemy. Classic is the game without any of it. See [Playing alone](game-modes.md#playing-alone).

## First steps

1. Put up a [Watchtower](playing/territory.md#land-claims) or two at the edge of what you want. Claimed land is land nobody else can build on.
2. Build a [Weaponsmith](buildings/weaponsmith.md) and a [Barracks](buildings/barracks.md). Spearmen are free to train. Archers need an [Archery Range](buildings/archery-range.md).
3. Scout the [bandit camps](playing/bandits.md) near you. They start raiding in cycle 7, or the cycle set in [Bandit raids](settings.md#bandit-raids).
4. Pick a [district specialization](playing/economy.md#district-specializations) once you know what each district is for.
