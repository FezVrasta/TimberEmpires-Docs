# Playing

Timber Empires keeps Timberborn's economy and puts players on the same map as rivals. Most of it comes down to a few things:

- [Territory](territory.md): who owns what, land claims, borders and fog of war.
- [Alliances](alliances.md): allying with other players in PvP.
- [Economy](economy.md): science per player, specializations and trading with other players.
- [Rivers](rivers.md): dams under pressure, water mines and fireships.
- [Bandits](bandits.md) and [Victory](victory.md).

The fighting is in [Military](../military/index.md).
