# Victory

The host's [Victory](../settings.md#victory) setting decides how a game is won:

- **Kill all the beavers.** You're out when none of your districts has a beaver left, adult or child.
- **Destroy the District Centers** (the default). You're out when you have no District Center left, built or under construction.
- **Build the wonder first.** The first player to launch their faction's wonder wins.

In the first two, the last player standing wins. A player who's out stays out.

Only players who have owned a district count, and it takes at least two of them, so there's no victory playing alone. Once someone wins everyone is told, and everyone can keep playing.
