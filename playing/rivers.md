# Rivers

## Dam pressure

With [Dam pressure](../settings.md#dam-pressure) on, water pushes on everything that holds it back: dams, levees and floodgates, and walls too (palisades, walls, gatehouses, wall towers and hoardings all hold water). Each can hold so much water per block of thickness (a dam or a wall the least, a floodgate the most), and it holds more when it's thicker (up to 4 blocks), when it stands on the ground, and when rock is close at its ends. Neighbouring blocks share the load, and flowing water pushes harder than still water.

Over its limit, a dam takes damage, faster the more it's overloaded, and at 100% it gives way. Under its limit it mends slowly, but not while it's under fire. The panel shows the water pressure as a share of its limit, and with the landscaping tools open dams are tinted green, yellow or red.

A battering ram's swings and water mines weaken dams too.

A wall built across a river, or used to flood an enemy, is a dam: too much water behind it and it gives way like one.

## Water mines

Main articles: [Mine Workshop](../buildings/mine-workshop.md), [Mine Slipway](../buildings/mine-slipway.md), [Moored Mine](../buildings/moored-mine.md), [Mine Net](../buildings/mine-net.md) and [Net Winch](../buildings/net-winch.md)
{: .hatnote}

The Mine Workshop makes water mines, and the Mine Slipway launches them into the current. They drift, arm after a short while, and go off when they hit something, or at the bottom of a waterfall. Moored mines sit on open water and go off when a boat or a drifting mine comes close. Mine nets catch drifting mines, and a Net Winch hauls the catch in for you to use.

**A blast** (1.6 tiles around):

- destroys small buildings (4 tiles or less) and damages bigger ones and walls;
- weakens dams;
- hurts beavers;
- sets off armed moored mines nearby, which can chain.

Roads aren't hurt.

## Fireships

Main article: [Fireship Dock](../buildings/fireship-dock.md)
{: .hatnote}

The Fireship Dock launches a boat upstream, packed with explosives. It blows up on the first building or mine it hits, and sinks near a water source or at the map's edge.
