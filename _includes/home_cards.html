TerritoryDistricts belong to players. Watchtowers claim land, fog of war hides the rest. TroopsSpearmen, archers and each faction's own, commanded like an RTS. WallsPalisades, gatehouses, ballista towers, and hoardings that roll logs or pour oil. SiegeCatapults, trebuchets, rams, siege towers and ladders, crewed by your troops. AlliancesAlly with other players in PvP, or play PvE all against the bandits. MonksThey convert enemy troops and machines to your side. RiversDams that give way, drifting water mines, mine nets and fireships. TradeTrading posts, power exchanges and toll stations between players. BanditsCamps on every map that raid whoever leaves them alone.
