# Game modes

A game has a mode, picked on the last page of the New Game screens, in the column between the difficulties and their details. BeaverBuddies ships Co-op, and Timber Empires adds PvE, PvP and Horde. The mode is saved with the game, and players who join get the host's.

| Mode | Players | Against | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Co-op](#co-op) | 2 or more, one settlement | Nobody | None, just build |
| [PvE](#pve) | 1 or more, allied | The bandits | Clear every bandit camp |
| [PvP](#pvp) | 1 or more | Each other and the bandits | The [Victory](playing/victory.md) setting |
| [Horde](#horde) | 1 or more | Each other and the undead raccoons | Seal every ruin, and outlast your rivals |

Alone you can pick PvE, PvP, Horde or Classic: see [Playing alone](#playing-alone).

## Co-op

Everyone builds one settlement together. Nobody owns districts, so there's no fog of war, no land claims and no border protection, and science is shared. No bandits, no victory.

## PvE

Each player owns their districts, with their own land claims and science, and every player is everyone's ally against the bandits. See [Alliances](playing/alliances.md#what-allies-share) for what allies share.

The bandits camp around the map and raid each player from the [first raid](settings.md#bandit-raids) cycle. Clear every camp and everyone wins together. You can keep playing after.

## PvP

Everyone for themselves. Each player owns their districts and everything on the [Territory](playing/territory.md) page applies. Players can make [alliances](playing/alliances.md), and the bandits raid everyone. The [Victory](playing/victory.md) setting decides how it's won.

## Horde

A PvP match where the undead raccoons climb out of the ruins every few nights and come for everyone. Every horde you survive pays out goods, buildings and siege machines you can use right away. Seal every ruin to win. With rivals in the game, you also have to outlast them. With [Shared colony](settings.md#shared-colony) on, everyone builds one colony together. See [Horde](playing/horde.md).

## Hosting and matchmaking

- **Hosting a new game.** Multiplayer, Host new game, then the New Game screens as usual, picking the mode on the last page. On a map with one start, players take districts from the District Center's "Owned by" in its panel, or build their own. Each player plays their own faction, and a player whose faction isn't on the map yet places a free first District Center.
- **Quick match.** Pairs you with another player over Steam who picked the same mode. Each of you picks a faction, the host's map is used, and both players place their own first District Center anywhere on it: it's built at once, with the beavers, food and water the game's difficulty starts you with. Missing mods are downloaded from the Workshop before the match starts.
- **Open matches.** The list shows each match's game mode.
- **Starting.** The game starts when the host first unpauses it. That's when the AI opponents found and the bandit camps go down.

## Playing alone

Pick PvE, PvP, Horde or Classic. PvE, PvP and Horde need [BeaverBuddies Co-Op/PvP Edition](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813984327) installed, even though nobody joins: without it there are no owned districts or fog of war. Start without waiting for anyone.

**PvE.** Your colony against the bandits. Clear every bandit camp to win.

**PvP.** Your empire against the bandits. Add [AI opponents](ai-opponents.md) in the game settings and they're your rivals: destroy their District Centers to win. Without rivals there's no victory, so it's a sandbox for the military side of the mod.

**Horde.** The undead raccoons come every few nights, with AI opponents beside you if you add them. See [Horde](playing/horde.md).

In all three, your districts are yours: land claims, fog of war and owner colors all work, and the fog hides the bandit camps until you scout them.

**Classic.** The game as it comes, with Timber Empires' buildings, troops, walls and siege machines added. There's no ownership, no land claims, no fog of war and no raids.
