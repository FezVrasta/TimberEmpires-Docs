# Monk

Monks convert enemies instead of fighting. Every faction trains them at the [Monastery](../buildings/monastery.md).

## Converting

A monk converts another player's troops, carters and siege machines out in the open. Bandits can't be converted.

Send a monk at an enemy and it starts chanting once close enough. Nothing happens in the first few minutes, then a conversion gets likelier the longer it goes on, and it's certain after about half an hour. Moving out of reach starts it over. After a conversion the monk's faith has to refill before it can convert again (the panel shows "Faith").

Monks need books (Iron Teeth: coffee) to convert and start with none, so supply them before sending them out.

**A converted troop** joins the nearest of its new owner's barracks that trains its kind, fully trained and keeping its rank. With no such barracks, it becomes an ordinary beaver.

**A converted machine** changes owner and throws out its crew.

Both sides get a notification.
