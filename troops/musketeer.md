# Musketeer

Shoots further and harder than an [archer](archer.md). Trained at the [Archery Range](../buildings/archery-range.md) by both factions, with a musket from the [Arms Workshop](../buildings/arms-workshop.md) (Folktails) or the [Gunsmith](../buildings/gunsmith.md) (Iron Teeth). Muskets are only made on Archipelago maps.
