# Sapper

{% include infobox-troop.html id="sapper" %}

The Iron Teeth wall breaker. Sappers throw blasting charges at buildings, walls and machines, and hurt troops around them. They go for buildings first. Blasting charges come from the [Gunsmith](../buildings/gunsmith.md).
