Folktails troops walk 10% faster and hit about 6% harder. Iron Teeth troops take 5% less damage. Factions from other mods train their base faction's troops, or the ones their mod picks.

## Training

A Barracks or Archery Range is a workplace: its workers are your troops. Pick which kind it trains in its panel. Spearmen and archers are free; the other kinds are unlocked from the panel with science. See [Barracks](../buildings/barracks.md).

Troops don't need roads: they walk anywhere, and climb steps with your [Field Ladders](../buildings/field-ladder.md).

## Weapons

Every troop needs its weapon, and a barracks keeps 6 in stock for its recruits. Your haulers bring them from where they're made:

- [Forge](../buildings/forge.md). Swords, shields, spears and bows, and slings for Folktails.
- [Armory](../buildings/armory.md) (Folktails). Battle axes.
- [Gunsmith](../buildings/gunsmith.md) (Iron Teeth). Arquebuses and blasting charges.
- Herbalist (Folktails, the game's own). Herbal kits.

Monks need no weapon: each one costs 40 science to train, and books (Iron Teeth: coffee) to keep working.

Gear wears out in two days of use, and troops swap in a spare from their kit (see [Supply](../military/combat.md#supply)).
