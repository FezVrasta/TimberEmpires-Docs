Folktails troops walk faster and hit harder. Iron Teeth troops take less damage. Factions from other mods train their base faction's troops, or the ones their mod picks.

## Training

A Barracks or Archery Range is a workplace: its workers are your troops. Pick which kind it trains in its panel. Spearmen, archers, militia and scouts are free; the other kinds are unlocked from the panel with science. See [Barracks](../buildings/barracks.md).

Troops don't need roads, and climb steps with your [Field Ladders](../buildings/field-ladder.md).

## Weapons

Troops need their weapon (militia and scouts have none), and a barracks keeps 6 in stock for its recruits. Your haulers bring them from where they're made:

- [Weaponsmith](../buildings/weaponsmith.md). Spears, bows and shields, plus swords for Iron Teeth and slings for Folktails.
- [Arms Workshop](../buildings/arms-workshop.md) (Folktails). Swords and battle axes, and muskets on Archipelago maps.
- [Gunsmith](../buildings/gunsmith.md) (Iron Teeth). Arquebuses and blasting charges, and muskets on Archipelago maps.
- Herbalist (Folktails, the game's own). Herbal kits.

Monks need no weapon. Each one costs 40 science to train, and books (Iron Teeth: coffee) to keep working.

Gear wears out after two days of use, and troops swap in a spare from their kit (see [Supply](../military/combat.md#supply)).
