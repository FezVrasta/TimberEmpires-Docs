# Settings

Timber Empires' settings are in Mod Settings, on the main menu and in the game. In a multiplayer game some are the host's call: the host's value is sent to everyone and saved with the game, and yours doesn't matter. The rest only change your own game.

| Setting | Default | Whose |
|---|---|---|
| [Separate science per player](#separate-science-per-player) | On | Host |
| [Border protection](#border-protection) | On | Host |
| [District specializations](#district-specializations) | On | Host |
| [Build roads and new districts](#build-roads-and-new-districts) | On | Host |
| [Dam pressure](#dam-pressure) | On | Host |
| [Mixed factions](#mixed-factions) | Off | Host |
| [Victory](#victory) | Destroy the District Centers | Host |
| [Bandit camps on new maps](#bandit-camps-on-new-maps) | On | Host |
| [Bandit raids](#bandit-raids) | On | Host |
| [Fog of war](#fog-of-war) | On | Host |
| [Top bar shows my districts](#top-bar-shows-my-districts) | On | Host |
| [My faction](#my-faction) | Host's faction | Yours |
| [Play alone as an empire](#play-alone-as-an-empire) | On | Yours |
| [Show district owner colors](#show-district-owner-colors) | On | Yours |
| [Battle music](#battle-music) | On | Yours |

In single player the host settings are yours.

## Separate science per player

Each player has their own science (in Co-op, science is always shared). Districts earn it for their owner, and whoever unlocks something pays for it and gets it only for themselves. Districts nobody owns earn for a shared pool, which anyone can spend once their own points run out.

## Border protection

Nobody can demolish, pause or change the settings of buildings and beavers in someone else's districts.

## District specializations

Each district can pick a production focus. See [District specializations](playing/economy.md#district-specializations).

## Build roads and new districts

Builders lay roads (they cost nothing but a builder's time), and every District Center after your first is a real building: 100 logs, 120 planks, 60 gears, 30 metal blocks and 800 science. Off, roads and District Centers appear at once and for free, as in the base game.

## Dam pressure

Water pushes on dams, levees, floodgates and walls, and thin, tall or unbraced ones give way. See [Dam pressure](playing/rivers.md#dam-pressure).

## Mixed factions

Both factions are loaded, and each player builds with their own. It's read when a multiplayer game loads and saved with it, so set it before hosting. Matchmade games always allow mixing.

## Victory

How a PvP game is won: kill all the beavers, destroy the District Centers, or build the wonder first. PvE and Co-op have their own goals. See [Victory](playing/victory.md).

## Bandit camps on new maps

Places bandit camps when a new game starts on a map that has none (never in Co-op). See [Bandits](playing/bandits.md).

## Bandit raids

From the third cycle, camps raid the players near them. Turning it off cancels planned raids; one already under way still plays out.

## My faction

The faction you build with in a mixed-factions game: the host's, or any faction installed, other mods' too. It takes effect when the game loads.

## Play alone as an empire

In single player, your districts are yours and land claims and fog of war work as they would against other players. See [Playing alone](getting-started.md#playing-alone).

## Fog of war

In a game with others you only see around your land, buildings, beavers and troops. It only changes what players see, but it's the host's call, so nobody can lift it for themselves. See [Fog of war](playing/territory.md#fog-of-war).

## Top bar shows my districts

With no district selected, the top bar counts only your districts' goods and beavers, not the whole map's.

## Show district owner colors

Tints each player's District Centers, roofs, paths and banners in their color. It only changes what you see.

## Battle music

Plays battle music while your troops or beavers fight, and goes back to the game's music 20 seconds after the last blow.

## Licenses and credits

A button that opens the mod's license and credits. See [Credits](credits.md).
