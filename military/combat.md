# Combat

## Health and damage

Every troop has 100 health and deals its damage over time while an enemy is in reach. What a blow does depends on:

- **Strong against.** Spearmen beat warriors, archers and slingers beat spearmen, arquebusiers beat berserkers. See [Troops](../reference/troops.md).
- **Gear.** A worn weapon (a quarter or less left) hits half as hard. Gear wears out after two days of use, and troops swap in a spare from their kit.
- **Shields.** A warrior's shield cuts the damage from shots until it's worn down.
- **High ground.** More damage from above, less from below, and shooters reach further from above.
- **Walls.** Shots can't pass through buildings or terrain, and walls block melee blows. Troops on a wall take less from shots. Troops that can't reach an enemy break down the wall in their way.
- **Ranks.** See below.
- **Toughness.** Each kind takes more or less damage, listed in [Troops](../reference/troops.md).

Shooters stopped by an enemy on the march hit half as hard.

## Attacking buildings

With no enemy in reach, troops go for enemy buildings that fight back: siege machines, towers armed with one, hoardings and manned towers. Any other building they only attack when you send them at it. Bandits and AI opponents break through whatever is in their way. Sappers go for buildings first.

Troops can't hit a building through a wall. Construction sites can be attacked too.

Siege shots, logs and boiling oil ignore shields, ranks and wall cover.

## Building in a fight

Nobody builds or demolishes in the middle of a fight. You can't place a building within 12 tiles of an enemy's building or fighter, or demolish one of your own with an enemy fighter that close. [Field Ladders](../buildings/field-ladder.md) and [Field Ramps](../buildings/field-ramp.md) are the exceptions: troops can put them up right next to the enemy.

## Ranks

Troops earn points for every enemy they bring down, more for a dangerous one and less the higher their own rank. They climb from Veteran to Elite to Hero, and each rank adds damage and cuts the damage they take (numbers in [Troops](../reference/troops.md#ranks)). A gold bar under the health bar shows the rank. It's kept when a troop goes home or is converted.

## Healing

- **Rest.** Troops heal 5 health an hour while fed and watered, starting 45 minutes after their last hit.
- **[Herbalists](../troops/herbalist.md)** (Folktails) heal the most hurt troop near them, yours or an ally's, 40 an hour, and walk to hurt troops close by.
- **[Field Infirmary](../buildings/field-infirmary.md)** (Iron Teeth). A healing tub on wheels. It treats one troop at a time, using extract and berries. Hurt troops nearby go to it when no enemy is close.

## Needs

Troops get hungry and thirsty faster than other beavers, and tire much more slowly. Out on orders they sleep where they stand instead of going home. Aboard a machine their needs and gear don't wear down.

## Supply

Each troop carries a kit: three days of food and water, and two spares of each piece of gear. They restock at home, or out in the field when it's almost empty.

**[Wagon Yard](../buildings/wagon-yard.md).** Sends supply carts into the field, loaded with food, water, siege stones, ballista bolts, gears and building materials (logs, planks, metal blocks). Troops nearby eat, drink and take spare gear from it (allies' troops too), and build siege machines and ladders with its materials. A carter that gets tired or hungry parks the cart and goes home, and the next carter picks it up.

**Building in the field.** Troops build Timber Empires' military buildings wherever they are. Field ladders and ramps are built only by troops.
