# Walls

**Palisade** (Folktails) and **Wall** (Iron Teeth). Blocks everyone, friend or foe. Nothing can be built on top of it but siege ladders. Click a wall with troops selected and they line up on its walkway (or press N for the nearest).

**Gatehouse.** Lets your side and your [allies](../playing/alliances.md) through, and shuts when anyone else comes within 5 tiles: enemy troops and workers, bandits. It opens again half an hour after the last of them is more than 7 tiles away. The game's own Closed and Automated gate modes still work. Only the passage shuts; the stairs and the wall walk stay open.

**Wall Tower.** A strong point in the wall that sees 8 tiles around. Two versions carry a machine on top:

- **Ballista Tower.** Shoots enemy troops, or failing that enemy machines, from 2 to 25 tiles. Needs one troop aboard, takes two.
- **Trebuchet Tower.** Throws siege stones from 9 to 36 tiles, hitting everything around where they land. Needs two troops aboard, takes three.

Both pick their targets on their own while crewed.

**Hoardings.** A gallery on the wall that drops things on attackers right below, on your command: the button in its panel, or an automation signal. Up to 10 drops can be queued. They need a troop aboard, and a second one reloads faster.

- **Log Hoarding** (Folktails). Rolls 5 logs out from the wall, each hitting everyone in its lane. A log that hits an enemy machine damages it.
- **Oil Hoarding** (Iron Teeth). Pours boiling canola oil on the spot below, hurting everyone there, machines too.

Both only reach enemies at about the level of the wall's foot.

## Ammunition

Trebuchet Towers and hoardings use ammunition: siege stones, logs or canola oil. Their crew fetch it, see [Ammunition](siege.md#ammunition).

## Damage and repairs

Every building can be damaged and brought down. Walls, towers and machines have their own health (see [Walls and machines](../reference/machines.md)). Other buildings have 40 health per tile, between 60 and 400, and a District Center has 2300.

Walls hold back water, so with [Dam pressure](../settings.md#dam-pressure) on, a wall with too much water behind it takes damage and can give way like a dam (see [Rivers](../playing/rivers.md#dam-pressure)).

Builders repair damaged buildings between jobs, starting an hour after the last hit. Construction priority decides which come first.

While a building is under attack you get an alert and a war horn.

The **Watchtower** sends an automation signal while enemy troops or bandits stand on its land, to close gates or start hoardings on its own.
