# Military

## Troop kinds

| | Folktails | Iron Teeth |
|---|---|---|
| Barracks | Spearman, Warrior, Herbalist, Berserker | Spearman, Warrior, Sapper |
| Archery Range | Archer, Slinger | Archer, Arquebusier |
| Monastery | Monk | Monk |

- **Spearman.** Steady melee, long reach. Strong against warriors and the best at breaking siege machines.
- **Warrior.** Hits hard up close. Can carry a shield, which turns aside arrows and shots.
- **Archer.** Shoots from range. Strong against spearmen.
- **Herbalist** (Folktails). Never fights: heals the most hurt troop near it.
- **Berserker** (Folktails). The toughest melee troop.
- **Slinger** (Folktails). Quick, cheap, longer range than an archer. Strong against spearmen.
- **Sapper** (Iron Teeth). Throws blasting charges at buildings, walls and machines, and hurts troops around them.
- **Arquebusier** (Iron Teeth). The longest range, strong against berserkers.
- **Monk.** Converts enemy troops and machines. See [Monks](monks.md).

Folktails troops walk 10% faster and hit about 6% harder. Iron Teeth troops take 5% less damage. The numbers are in [Troops](../reference/troops.md).

Factions from other mods train their base faction's troops, or the ones their mod picks.

## Training troops

A Barracks or Archery Range is a workplace: its workers are your troops. Pick which kind it trains in its panel. Spearmen and archers are free; the other kinds are unlocked from the barracks panel with science. Changing the kind lets the current troops go, and new recruits come in to train as the new kind.

A recruit picks up a weapon from the barracks, then trains inside for a few hours (see [Troops](../reference/troops.md)). Recruits can't be ordered around and don't fight. A troop that's let go gives its weapon back. Barracks never take beavers sick with badwater, and a troop that catches it goes home and leaves.

Troops don't need roads: they walk anywhere, and climb steps with your siege ladders (see [Getting around](controls.md#getting-around)).

**Rally point.** "Set rally point" in the barracks panel sends each new troop to a spot of your choosing once trained.

## Weapons

Every troop needs its weapon, and a barracks keeps 6 in stock for its recruits. Your haulers bring them from where they're made:

- **Forge.** Swords, shields, spears and bows, and slings for Folktails.
- **Armory** (Folktails). Battle axes.
- **Gunsmith** (Iron Teeth). Arquebuses and blasting charges.
- **Herbalist** (Folktails). Herbal kits.

Monks need no weapon: each one costs 40 science to train, and books (Iron Teeth: coffee) to keep working.

A barracks shows "No weapons" or "No haulers for weapons" when its recruits have been waiting an hour.
