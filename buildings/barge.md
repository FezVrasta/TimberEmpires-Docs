# Barge

A cargo boat with a big open hold. Pick what it loads and unloads, and give it a route of [Trade Posts](trade-post.md) to sail. The fuller the hold, the slower it sails and turns.

Workers sail it. With Timber Empires you can switch its crew to troops.
