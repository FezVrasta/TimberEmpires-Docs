# Field Ladder

Lets troops and carters climb a one-level step in the ground, up or down. They use ladders on their own, picking the one that leaves them closest to where they're going. Your troops and your [allies'](../playing/alliances.md#what-allies-share) climb your ladders. Only troops can build one, and it can go up right beside enemies.

Machines can't climb a ladder: they need a [Field Ramp](field-ramp.md). Getting over a wall takes a [Siege Tower](siege-tower.md).
