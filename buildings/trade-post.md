# Trade Post

Ships moored at its pier trade goods, and ships on a trade route stop here. Set what it buys and sells and what each good is worth: with logs at 2 and water at 4, one water goes for two logs. Your own ships trade at your posts for free, and other players' posts trade by their values.

Build it on a shore with the pier in the water.
