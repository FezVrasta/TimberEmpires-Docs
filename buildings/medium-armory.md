# Medium Armory

{% include infobox-building.html id="medium-armory" %}

A warehouse for weapons and ammunition only, one tile wide and three deep, with its door on the short front. It keeps one good, picked among troops' weapons and shields and what machines fire, holds 90 of it and stacks. It's in the Storage tab.
