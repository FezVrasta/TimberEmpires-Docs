# Trading Post

Trades goods with another player. It's built across the border between your district and theirs, in pairs like a District Crossing.

Each side's owner sets, per good, whether their half buys it, sells it or leaves it, and what it's worth: with logs at 2 and water at 4, one water goes for two logs. Goods cross when both halves' values agree and both have workers. Between two districts of the same player they cross for free.

The Trades tab of the settlement panel lists every post you're part of.
