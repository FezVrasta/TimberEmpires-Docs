# Shipyard

Builds every ship on an [Archipelago](../archipelago.md) map. Put it on a shore with its dock in the shallows, then place a ship on the dock. Its workers also repair damaged ships that sail back to it.

Keep the water in front of it clear, or ships can't come and go.
