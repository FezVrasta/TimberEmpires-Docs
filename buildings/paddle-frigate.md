# Paddle Frigate

The Iron Teeth war boat, driven by paddle wheels and a boiler instead of the wind. It carries [troops](../troops/index.md) and has guns, like the [War Catamaran](war-catamaran.md). Build it on a [Shipyard](shipyard.md)'s dock.
