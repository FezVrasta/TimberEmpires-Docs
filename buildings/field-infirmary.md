# Field Infirmary

{% include infobox-building.html id="field-infirmary" %}

An Iron Teeth healing tub on wheels for after a battle. It treats one troop at a time, 60 health an hour, using a dose of 1 extract and 2 berries. Troops below 90% health within 10 tiles go to it when no enemy is close.

Its crew only pushes it around. Only troops can build it.
