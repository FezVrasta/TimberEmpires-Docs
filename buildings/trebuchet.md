# Trebuchet

{% include infobox-building.html id="trebuchet" %}

Brings down walls and buildings from far away, and hurts every enemy near where its stones land. It doesn't move. Unlike the other siege machines, your builders put it up, not your troops.

Select it and click an enemy to aim it. It turns to face the target and fires until nothing is left to hit. Shots land a little off target. It needs troops aboard to turn and throw (see [Crew](../military/siege.md#crew)), and siege stones from the [Stone Press](stone-press.md).
