# Trebuchet

Knocks down walls and buildings from far away, and hurts every enemy near where its stones land. It can't move, but you can build it on a [Round Tower](round-tower.md).

Select it and click an enemy to aim. It keeps firing until there's nothing left to hit, and shots land a little off target. It needs troops aboard to turn and fire (see [Crew](../military/siege.md#crew)), and siege stones from the [Stone Press](stone-press.md).
