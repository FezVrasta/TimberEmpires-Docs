# Mine Net

{% include infobox-building.html id="mine-net" %}

Floats in the river and catches drifting mines, two per net. Lay several end to end to make a barrier. A full net lets them drift on, or they blow up against it.

A [Net Winch](net-winch.md) empties the nets.
