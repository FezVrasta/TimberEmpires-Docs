# Toll Station

{% include infobox-building.html id="toll-station" %}

Lets your beavers ride another player's ziplines for a toll. You build it on their network, and your beavers get on and off only at toll stations. The network's owner sets the toll, or makes it free, and can close the station. Each rider pays when boarding; the owner's haulers collect the tolls.

Only one other district can ride a given network at a time. Connected to its own district's network, a toll station is a plain station.
