# Trebuchet Tower

{% include infobox-building.html id="trebuchet-tower" %}

A [Wall Tower](wall-tower.md) with a small trebuchet on its deck. With a crew aboard it throws siege stones at enemies on its own, from 9 to 36 tiles, hitting everything around where they land.

It needs two troops aboard and takes three. The crew fetch the stones themselves from stores within 12 tiles (see [Ammunition](../military/siege.md#ammunition)), and stones come from the [Stone Press](stone-press.md).
