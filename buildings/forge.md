# Forge

{% include infobox-building.html id="forge" %}

Makes the basic gear troops train and fight with: swords, shields, spears and bows, and slings for Folktails. It burns logs.

Your haulers take the gear to the barracks that need it. Recipes are in [Weapons and goods](../reference/goods.md).
