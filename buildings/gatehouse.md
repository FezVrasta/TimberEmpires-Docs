# Gatehouse

{% include infobox-building.html id="gatehouse" %}

The way through a wall. By default it lets your side and your [allies](../playing/alliances.md) pass, and shuts when anyone else comes within 5 tiles: enemy troops and workers, and bandits. It opens again half an hour after the last of them is more than 7 tiles away.

Only the passage shuts. The stairs up to the wall walk stay open, so a gatehouse is also how your troops get onto the wall.

A [Battering Ram](battering-ram.md) is the machine for breaking one.

## Gate modes

Its panel has four modes, and only its owner can change them:

- **Auto open** (default). Open for your side, shut when enemies or bandits come near, as above.
- **Force open.** Open for everyone, enemies too.
- **Closed.** Shut for everyone.
- **Automated.** Opens and shuts on an automation signal, and still shuts when enemies come near. Wired to a [Watchtower](watchtower.md), it closes before anyone gets close.
