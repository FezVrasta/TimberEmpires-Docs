# Battering Ram

The Folktails machine for breaking gates, walls and buildings. Its crew pushes it up to the target and swings it, faster with more troops aboard.

Each swing also takes a fifth off a dam's strength.

Enemy [spearmen](../troops/spearman.md) are the best answer to a ram, and [hoardings](log-hoarding.md) hit it when it rolls up to the wall.
