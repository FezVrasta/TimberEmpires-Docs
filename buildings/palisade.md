# Palisade

{% include infobox-building.html id="palisade" %}

The Folktails wall. It blocks everyone, friend or foe: a [Gatehouse](gatehouse.md) lets your side through.

## Manning the wall

Click a wall with troops selected and they line up on its walkway around that spot and hold there. N sends the selected troops to the nearest wall within 30 tiles. Troops on a wall take 20% less from shots, and melee blows can't reach through it.

## Holding water

A palisade holds back water like a dam. With [Dam pressure](../settings.md#dam-pressure) on, too much water behind it and it gives way (see [Dam pressure](../playing/rivers.md#dam-pressure)).
