# Drawbridge Gatehouse

{% include infobox-building.html id="drawbridge-gatehouse" %}

A [Gatehouse](gatehouse.md) with a bridge across a ditch or a moat. The bridge reaches two tiles out from the gate and goes up whenever the gate shuts, so it opens and shuts on the same rules and panel modes.

It stands on its own foundation row, so its passage is one level up: walls that join it need a [Wall Foundation](wall-foundation.md) under them. The two tiles under the bridge must be empty, and stay that way.

A raised bridge doesn't keep the gate safe. Enemies on the far side can still batter it from across the gap.
