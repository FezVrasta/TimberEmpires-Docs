# Weaponsmith

{% include infobox-building.html id="weaponsmith" %}

Makes the basic gear troops train and fight with, and the bolts ballistas shoot: spears, bows, shields and ballista bolts, plus swords for Iron Teeth and slings for Folktails. It burns logs. It used to be called the Forge.

Folktails make their swords at the [Bladesmith](bladesmith.md).

Your haulers take the gear to the barracks that need it, and the bolts to [ballistas](ballista.md) and [Ballista Towers](ballista-tower.md). Each batch makes 5 bolts. Recipes are in [Weapons and goods](../reference/goods.md).
