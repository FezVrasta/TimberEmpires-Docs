# Cog

A fast ship with six gun ports and a hold for cargo. Build it on a [Shipyard](shipyard.md)'s dock. Its guns fire only with a military crew: troops sail it and man them.

A ship with a civil crew that lands on a coast away from your roads founds a settlement there.
