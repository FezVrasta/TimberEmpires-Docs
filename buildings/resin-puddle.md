# Resin Puddle

Pine resin poured on the ground or a road, to set alight under attackers. It burns for 6 hours and hurts everyone standing in it, your own beavers too.

Light it with a [Resin Igniter](resin-igniter.md), or send archers at it with the attack cursor: only archers on a wall within 2 pieces of a lit [Blazer](blazer.md) shoot fire arrows.
