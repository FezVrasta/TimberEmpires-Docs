# Moored Mine

{% include infobox-building.html id="moored-mine" %}

A mine anchored on open water, armed by a [Mine Slipway](mine-slipway.md)'s boat. It goes off when a boat or a drifting mine comes close, when another blast reaches it, or when its water drains.
