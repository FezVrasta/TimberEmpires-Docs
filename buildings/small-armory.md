# Small Armory

{% include infobox-building.html id="small-armory" %}

A warehouse for weapons and ammunition only. It keeps one good, picked among troops' weapons and shields and what machines fire, holds 30 of it and stacks like the game's small warehouse. It's in the Storage tab.

Put one next to a tower or a line of siege engines and their crews fetch their ammunition from it (see [Ammunition](../military/siege.md#ammunition)).
