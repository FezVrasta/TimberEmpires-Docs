# Blazer

{% include infobox-building.html id="blazer" %}

A fire basket hung off the side of a wall, burning pine resin. While it's lit, your archers on the wall within 2 pieces of it shoot fire arrows, which set [Resin Puddles](resin-puddle.md) alight. Now and then one burns the archer.
