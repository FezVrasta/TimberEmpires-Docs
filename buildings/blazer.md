# Blazer

A fire basket hung off the side of a wall, burning pine resin that your haulers bring. While it's lit, archers on the wall within 2 wall pieces of it shoot fire arrows, which set [Resin Puddles](resin-puddle.md) alight. Now and then one burns the archer.
