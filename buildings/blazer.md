# Blazer

{% include infobox-building.html id="blazer" %}

A fire basket hung off the side of a wall, burning pine resin. Your archers on the wall within 2 joined pieces of a lit blazer shoot fire arrows, which set [Resin Puddles](resin-puddle.md) alight. Each fire arrow has a 1 in 10 chance of burning the archer for 15 health.

It burns a pine resin every 20 hours and holds 4. Haulers and builders reach it from the foot of the wall, on the side it hangs over.

It hangs off a palisade or wall piece, on the side you turn it to, and comes down with that piece. Gatehouses, towers and hoardings don't take one.
