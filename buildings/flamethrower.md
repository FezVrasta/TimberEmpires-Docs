# Flamethrower

Burns the [horde](../playing/horde.md) in a cone in front of it. It's only in the toolbar in Horde games, and it can also come as a reward.

It aims at the thickest crowd by itself, and only fires when at least 12 raccoons are in the cone. Its crew moves it and turns it, and it has to stand still to fire. On a [Round Tower](round-tower.md) it reaches further and turns faster.

Extract is its ammunition, fetched like any [siege machine's](../military/siege.md): it burns 15 an hour of firing, so a full load lasts about two hours. In Horde games [supply carts](wagon-yard.md) carry extract for it too.
