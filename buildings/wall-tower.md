# Wall Tower

{% include infobox-building.html id="wall-tower" %}

A strong point in the wall. It joins walls on any side, leads up to the wall walk and sees around it through the [fog of war](../playing/territory.md#fog-of-war), further from high ground.

For a siege machine on top, build a [Round Tower](round-tower.md).
