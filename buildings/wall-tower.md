# Wall Tower

{% include infobox-building.html id="wall-tower" %}

A strong point in the wall. It joins walls on any side, leads up to the wall walk and sees 8 tiles around through the [fog of war](../playing/territory.md#fog-of-war), further from high ground.

Two versions carry a machine on top: the [Ballista Tower](ballista-tower.md) and the [Trebuchet Tower](trebuchet-tower.md).
