# Barracks

Trains troops. Its workers are your troops: pick the kind in its panel. Spearmen are free, and the other kinds are unlocked there with science. Changing the kind lets the current troops go, and new recruits come in to train as the new kind.

## Training

A recruit takes a weapon from the barracks and trains inside for a few hours. The barracks keeps 6 weapons in stock, brought by your haulers from the [Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md), [Arms Workshop](arms-workshop.md) or [Gunsmith](gunsmith.md).

**Rally point.** "Set rally point" in the panel sends each new troop to a spot you pick once it's trained.
