# Watchtower

{% include infobox-building.html id="watchtower" %}

Claims land, so other players can't build on it. It claims the land around it, more the higher it stands.

It burns logs, and claims only while lit and connected to its district. Cut off, it goes out and keeps its logs.

While enemy troops or bandits stand on its land, it sends an automation signal: wire it to close a [Gatehouse](gatehouse.md) or start a [hoarding](log-hoarding.md) on its own.
