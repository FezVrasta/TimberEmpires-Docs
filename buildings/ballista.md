# Ballista

Shoots heavy bolts flat and far: deadly to troops, weak against walls. It rolls, and can go on a [Round Tower](round-tower.md).

Select it and click an enemy: it aims at the enemy closest to where you clicked and its crew pushes it into range. It doesn't fire while it moves. Its bolts come from the [Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md).
