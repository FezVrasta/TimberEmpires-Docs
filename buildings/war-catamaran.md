# War Catamaran

The Folktails war boat. It carries [troops](../troops/index.md) across the sea, and has guns of its own.

Place it on a [Shipyard](shipyard.md)'s slip. Once it's built and crewed, you send it to another island's coast to land the troops. [Pirates](../playing/bandits.md) sail the same kind of boat.
