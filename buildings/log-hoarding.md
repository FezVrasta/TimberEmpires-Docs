# Log Hoarding

{% include infobox-building.html id="log-hoarding" %}

A Folktails gallery on the wall that rolls logs down on attackers. It drops on your command: the button in its panel, or an automation signal. Up to 10 drops can be queued.

Each drop rolls 5 logs out from the wall, each hitting everyone in its lane. A log that hits an enemy machine damages it too. Logs ignore shields, ranks and wall cover.

It needs a troop aboard, and a second one reloads faster. The crew fetch the logs themselves (see [Ammunition](../military/siege.md#ammunition)).

It only reaches enemies at about the level of the wall's foot.
