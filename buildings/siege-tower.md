# Siege Tower

{% include infobox-building.html id="siege-tower" %}

The Iron Teeth way over a wall. Roll it up to a wall and unload: the crew step out onto the wall walk. Nobody climbs it while it moves. It climbs two levels, so it can't reach a wall standing higher than its top, on a [Wall Foundation](wall-foundation.md) or on higher ground. Only troops can build it.
