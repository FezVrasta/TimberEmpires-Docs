# Trip Wire

Build two posts and join them with a wire. Anyone who crosses it sends an automation signal from both ends. You pick who counts: enemies, allies and yours, only yours, or anybody.

Wire it to a [Gatehouse](gatehouse.md) or a [Resin Igniter](resin-igniter.md) to answer a raid before it reaches the wall.
