# Archipelago

Somewhere past the last shore there's another island, with its own trees, its own water and, sooner or later, its own beavers. Archipelago turns a map into islands drawn far apart on an open sea, and gives you the boats to get to them.

Timber Empires and BeaverBuddies use it when it's installed.

## The sea

Each island is a map area painted with the island brush. Between islands is open sea you can't build on, only cross.

## Ships

A [Shipyard](buildings/shipyard.md) builds every ship and mends the ones that come back battered.

## Trade

[Trade Posts](buildings/trade-post.md) let ships trade goods between islands, and a ship can follow a route of posts for as long as you let it.

## Storms

The storm is a hazardous season, like drought and badtide. Boats sail slower and roll more, and the sea turns dark.

## Pirates <span class="mod-badge mod-badge--te" title="From Timber Empires" role="img" aria-label="From Timber Empires"><span class="mod-badge-icon"></span></span>

With Timber Empires, bandit camps on the sea have [pirate boats](playing/bandits.md#pirates), and they don't ask what you're carrying.

## Modding

Other mods add ships with a blueprint, and hook into the sea through a small API. See [Modding](modding.md#archipelago-for-other-mods).
