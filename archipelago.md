# Archipelago

Archipelago draws a map's islands far apart on an open sea and gives you the boats to cross it. Each island has its own trees and water, and sooner or later its own beavers.

Timber Empires and BeaverBuddies use it when it's installed.

## The sea

Each island is a map area painted with the island brush. Between islands is open sea you can only cross.

## Ships

A [Shipyard](buildings/shipyard.md) builds every ship and repairs the ones that come back battered.

Send a finished ship to another island's coast to land there.

## Trade

[Trade Posts](buildings/trade-post.md) let ships trade goods between islands. Give a ship a route of posts and it sails it.

## Storms

The storm is a hazardous season, like drought and badtide: dark skies, rain and heavy seas. Boats sail slower and roll more.

## Pirates <span class="mod-badge mod-badge--te" title="From Timber Empires" role="img" aria-label="From Timber Empires"><span class="mod-badge-icon"></span></span>

With Timber Empires, bandit camps send [pirate boats](playing/bandits.md#pirates) to raid the coast.

## Modding

Other mods add ships with a blueprint, and hook into the sea through a small API. See [Modding with Archipelago](modding/archipelago.md).
